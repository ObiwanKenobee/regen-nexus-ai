# Regenerative Development Exchange (RDX)

> **Regenerative Economics meets AI Systems Thinking.**

The **Regenerative Development Exchange (RDX)** is an AI-driven innovation ecosystem designed to connect economic development, environmental regeneration, public policy, research, and community-led investment.

RDX combines:

```text
ECONOMICS
    +
ENVIRONMENTAL ENGINEERING
    +
POLITICAL ECONOMICS
    +
DEVELOPMENT MANAGEMENT
    +
URBAN & REGIONAL SYSTEMS
    +
AGRICULTURE & FORESTRY
    +
PUBLIC HEALTH
    +
AI SYSTEMS THINKING
```

into a shared infrastructure for climate-linked economic transformation.

The platform is designed to make development intelligence operational.

Instead of treating climate, capital, governance, education, health, and infrastructure as separate disciplines, RDX models them as an interconnected system.
